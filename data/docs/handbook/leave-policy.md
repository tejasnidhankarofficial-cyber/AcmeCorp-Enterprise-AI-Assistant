# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly.

## Sick leave
10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 days.

## Parental leave
16 weeks of paid parental leave for all new parents, taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to request
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 5 working days ahead; your manager approves them.

# Remote Work Policy (v3, effective 2026-01-01)

Acme Corp supports hybrid work. This version supersedes the 2022 policy.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

## Equipment
Acme provides a laptop and a one-time home-office stipend of $500. Equipment remains company property.

## Security
Remote employees must connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems and must keep
disk encryption enabled. See it/security-guidelines.md and it/vpn-setup.md.

## Exceptions
Full-remote arrangements require written approval from a department head and HR.
